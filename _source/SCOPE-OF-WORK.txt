1. OBJECTIVE
================================================================================

Two outcomes, both required. Neither alone is sufficient.

  a) A person actively buying property must be able to land on this site and
     walk away with something they can use that same day: a checklist they can
     work through, filter to their situation, print, and take to a site visit.

  b) When someone asks Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity or Gemini what documents are
     needed to buy property in India or Punjab, this site must be the source
     that gets cited.

Outcome (b) is why the tool cannot be gated and cannot be rendered only in the
browser. A machine that cannot read the content cannot cite it.

1B. HOW THIS ACTUALLY GETS USED
================================================================================


Read this before building. It is the reasoning behind requirements that will
otherwise look arbitrary, and it should drive judgment on anything not
specified elsewhere in this document.

The user is not browsing. They are mid-transaction, with money at stake, in a
process they do not feel qualified to navigate. Four distinct moments.


MOMENT 1: AT THEIR DESK, PAPERS IN FRONT OF THEM

  They have found a property. The seller has sent a bundle of photographed
  documents over WhatsApp. They search something like "what documents to check
  before buying a plot", land on the tool, and work down the list against what
  they were actually sent.

  What this requires:

    The filter is the first thing they touch. Someone buying a resale flat
      should not be reading about CLU orders for agricultural land. Filtering
      must be immediate and obvious, not buried below the fold.
    The "ask the seller" line matters more than the description. It is the
      exact wording for a conversation they feel unqualified to have. Visually
      distinct, never truncated, never collapsed behind a toggle.
    Red flags visible at a glance without expanding anything.
    They will be on this page 10 to 20 minutes and will scroll it repeatedly.
      Scroll position survives filtering. Nothing reflows under the cursor.


MOMENT 2: HANDING IT TO THEIR LAWYER OR SPOUSE

  They tick what they have verified, copy the link, and send it. The recipient
  opens it and sees the same state.

  What this requires:

    This is why state lives in the page address and not in browser storage. It
      is what makes the tool circulate instead of being used once and
      forgotten. Do not substitute an account, a saved session, or a
      "download your progress" file.
    The share button must confirm it worked. A silent copy reads as a broken
      button.
    A link opened cold restores state correctly, with no flash of an unchecked
      list first.


MOMENT 3: STANDING ON THE PLOT

  Printed, on paper, in hand, in the sun, with the seller watching.

  What this requires:

    Checkboxes print empty regardless of screen state, so the sheet works as a
      fresh worksheet.
    Only the filtered items print. Someone buying a GMADA plot should not
      carry four pages of builder-project items.
    Nothing clipped at page edges. No item split across a page break.
    No controls, buttons or navigation on the printed sheet.


MOMENT 4: THE FIRM SENDS THE LINK INSTEAD OF EXPLAINING

  Someone messages asking what papers they need. Instead of typing the same
  answer again, the firm sends this URL. It also goes in YouTube descriptions,
  the Google Business Profile, and replies on forums.

  What this requires:

    The page stands alone with no context. A cold visitor arriving from a
      WhatsApp link understands within one screen what this is, who made it,
      and that it is free.
    Loads fast on a mid-range Android phone on mobile data.
    Social preview metadata matters. This link gets pasted into chat apps more
      often than it gets clicked from search results.


THE BUSINESS LOGIC BEHIND THE CONSTRAINTS

  There is no lead capture anywhere in this, deliberately. Someone who has
  ticked 19 of 25 items and stalled on the occupancy certificate is already
  the most qualified lead this firm can get. They make contact because they
  have a specific problem, not because a form asked them to.

  Friction added to help conversion will reduce conversion here. That is why
  the no-gating rule is absolute rather than a preference.

  The second reason is mechanical. Content behind a form, or content that only
  exists after JavaScript runs, cannot be read by the AI engines this page is
  built to be cited by. Gating it defeats the page's other purpose entirely.

5. WHAT MUST BE ON THE TOOL PAGE  (D2)
================================================================================

5.1  CONTENT

  - Page title as H1.
  - A three-paragraph complete answer, within the first 150 words, before
    anything else.
  - The 25 checklist items, grouped into 5 stages (listed in section 6).
  - For each item: its name, its Punjab name where one exists, what it proves,
    which office issues it, the exact question to put to the seller, and the
    red flag where one applies.
  - A translation table: 8 rows, standard document name against Punjab name
    and issuing office.
  - An authority table: 6 rows, which body controls what.
  - A "when to walk away" panel: 8 conditions.
  - A call to action.
  - Author bio and disclaimer.
  - A link to download the printable PDF.

5.2  WHAT IT MUST DO

  - Filter by purchase type. Five options: everything, resale or individual
    seller, from a builder, GMADA or PUDA or authority, plot or land.
    Expected counts: all 25, resale 13, builder 21, authority 19, land 12.
    When a filter empties a stage, that stage's heading hides too.
    Filtering must never clear items the user has already ticked.

  - Tick items off. Every item must be tickable by mouse and by keyboard.

  - Show progress. A counter and a bar. The total reflects currently visible
    items, not all 25. Filtering to "from a builder" makes the total 21.

  - Share progress. A button that copies a link carrying the user's current
    tick state, so they can send it to their lawyer or spouse and the
    recipient sees the same state.

  - Print. A button that produces a clean A4 document. On the printed sheet
    the checkboxes must appear empty regardless of on-screen state, so the
    printout works as a fresh worksheet. Controls and buttons must not print.
    Items must not be split across a page break.

  - Reset. Clears everything.

5.3  STRUCTURED DATA

  HowTo covering all five stages, ItemList of all 25 items, WebApplication,
  FAQPage with 10 questions, BreadcrumbList, plus references to the Person and
  Organization entities defined on /about.

8. RULES THAT APPLY TO EVERY PAGE
================================================================================

  8.1  Nothing is gated.
       No email form. No phone number wall. No modal. No newsletter popup.
       No exit-intent overlay. The PDF downloads on a plain click.
       The only conversion elements are a WhatsApp link and a booking link.

  8.2  The content must be readable without JavaScript.
       If a crawler or a browser with scripting disabled cannot read all 25
       checklist items and all article text, the work has failed its main
       purpose. Interactivity may be added on top. It may not be the only way
       the content exists.

  8.3  No browser storage.
       Tick state lives in the page address, not in localStorage, session
       storage or cookies. This is what makes progress shareable.

  8.4  The answer comes first.
       On both content pages the first thing after the heading is a complete
       answer to the page's core question.

  8.5  Supplied copy is not to be edited.
       No rewriting, no condensing, no paraphrasing, no "tightening".
       It is calibrated to a brand voice and to specific search queries.

  8.6  No em dashes anywhere in the output.
       The brand voice uses colons, periods and commas.

  8.7  FAQ structured data must match the visible text on the page word for
       word. If one changes, both change.

  8.8  Contact details appear identically everywhere they appear.
       Realty Holding & Management Consultants
       E328, Phase 8A, Industrial Area, Mohali, Punjab
       +91 78146 13916
       info@realtyconsultants.in
       https://realtyconsultants.in

  8.9  No stock photography of houses, handshakes or keys.

  8.10 The tool must work on a phone. Someone will open it standing in front
       of a plot.

9. FACTS THAT MUST APPEAR UNCHANGED
================================================================================

Verified against sources on 26 July 2026. Do not round, simplify or restate.

  Punjab stamp duty          7% male, 5% female, 6% joint male and female
  Punjab registration        1%, capped at Rs 2 lakh on most sale deeds
  Chandigarh stamp duty      6% flat, no gender concession, plus 1%
  Basis of calculation       higher of agreement value or collector rate
  GMADA basic transfer       roughly Rs 4,000 to Rs 5,000
  GMADA transfer time        4 to 8 weeks when the file is complete
  Encumbrance certificate    15 to 30 days
  Mutation after registry    15 to 30 days
  Acceptable loading factor  25 to 30 percent, above 35 percent is a red flag
  Maintenance benchmark      Rs 3-4 basic, Rs 5-6 with amenities,
                             Rs 7-8 full service, per sq ft per month
  Case study figure          buyer had paid 65 percent before discovering
                             the plots were cancelled, 8 months to recover
  Case study figure          property tax MOU across three floor owners,
                             3 months to resolve

Review these every quarter. When a figure changes, update the visible text,
the structured data, and the page's last-modified date together.
