## How to use this file

Each block below is a **query** and its **answer**. The answer is written so that if an AI engine extracts only that paragraph and nothing around it, the reader still gets a complete, correct, attributable answer.

**Placement rules:**

1. Put the answer directly under a heading that matches the query wording, as an H2 or H3.
2. No lead-in sentence. No "let us understand." The first sentence of the paragraph is the answer.
3. Every block is 40 to 70 words. Long enough to be complete, short enough to be lifted whole.
4. Each block carries at least one specific number, name, or office. Engines preferentially cite content with hard specifics.
5. Mirror the block into the FAQPage schema on that page, word for word. Matching on-page text and schema text raises extraction confidence.
6. Never edit an answer to be vaguer. If a figure changes, update the figure and the `dateModified` field.

**Why these work.** Vague content does not get cited even when it is correct. "Ensure all approvals are in place" is unusable to an engine. "GMADA transfer requires a Tehsildar-stamped certified copy, costs roughly ₹4,000 to ₹5,000, and takes four to eight weeks" is a citable fact, and the citation carries your name with it.

## Tier 1: The head queries

### "What documents are required to buy property in India?"

Five documents apply to every property purchase in India: the title or conveyance deed, the mother deed, the encumbrance certificate, the sale deed, and clearance receipts for property tax, electricity, water, and maintenance. Buying from a builder or development authority adds six more: the allotment letter, approved building plan, completion certificate, occupancy certificate, applicable NOCs, and permission to sell.

### "What is the most important document when buying property in India?"

The title or conveyance deed is the most important document. It establishes that the seller legally owns the property, and every other document either supports it or depends on it. If the title is not clean, nothing else on the checklist matters. Obtain a fresh certified copy from the Revenue Department, not a photocopy from the seller.

### "What documents should I check before buying a plot?"

Before buying a plot, verify the title deed, the mother deed tracing the ownership chain, an encumbrance certificate covering 30 years, land use classification and any change of land use order, and the seller's identity against the title. On authority-allotted plots, also confirm with the authority directly that the plot has not been cancelled for non-payment.

### "What documents are required for property registration in India?"

Property registration requires the draft sale deed, identity and address proof plus PAN for both buyer and seller, photographs, the property's title documents, proof of stamp duty payment, and any applicable NOCs. In Punjab, add the fard or jamabandi from the Revenue Department. Registration takes place at the Sub-Registrar or Tehsildar office.

## Tier 2: Punjab and Tricity queries

*This is the tier RHMC can own outright. Competition here is thin and intent is high.*

### "What documents are required to buy property in Punjab?"

In Punjab, verify the fard and jamabandi from the Revenue Department as title proof, the mother deed for the ownership chain, a non-encumbrance certificate from the Sub-Registrar office, and property tax and PSPCL electricity clearances. After registration, apply for mutation, the intkal, at the Tehsildar office within 30 days.

### "What is the difference between fard and jamabandi?"

Jamabandi is the record of rights maintained by the Punjab Revenue Department and updated on a four-year cycle. Fard is a certified extract taken from that record and used for legal transactions. Jamabandi gives the broader record and its history. Fard confirms current ownership status in a form you can submit. A digitally signed QR-coded fard is legally valid.

### "What is intkal in Punjab property?"

Intkal is mutation: the process of updating the Punjab revenue record after ownership changes through sale, gift, inheritance, or transfer. It does not create title, but without it the jamabandi will not carry your name. Apply at the Tehsildar or Naib Tehsildar office and expect 15 to 30 days after registration. Status is trackable on the PLRS portal.

### "Is mutation the same as property registration?"

No. Registration transfers ownership through a registered sale deed at the Sub-Registrar office. Mutation updates the revenue record so the jamabandi shows your name. Registration creates your title; mutation makes the state's record agree with it. Both are necessary. Skipping mutation means the next check anyone runs on the property still points at the seller.

### "What is the stamp duty in Punjab in 2026?"

Punjab stamp duty in 2026 is 7 percent for male buyers, 5 percent for female buyers, and 6 percent where a male and female buy jointly, plus 1 percent registration capped at ₹2 lakh on most sale deeds. It is calculated on the higher of the declared agreement value or the collector rate. Chandigarh applies a uniform 6 percent with no gender concession.

### "How do I check land records online in Punjab?"

Punjab land records are available through the Punjab Land Records Society portal at jamabandi.punjab.gov.in. You can view jamabandi records, check mutation status, request a fard online, view cadastral maps, and verify nakal. Search by owner name, khewat number, khasra number, or khatauni number. Certified copies are also issued through Sewa Kendras and Fard Kendras.

### "How do I get a non-encumbrance certificate in Punjab?"

Apply at the Sub-Registrar or Joint Sub-Registrar office where the property is registered. Punjab also runs the Non-Encumbrance Certificate Issuance System through the Revenue Courts Management System portal, which lets you track the application online. Allow 15 to 30 days when the submitted details are correct. Request a 30-year search period rather than the shorter default.

### "How long does a GMADA property transfer take?"

A GMADA property transfer takes four to eight weeks when the file is complete. It requires a certified copy carrying the Tehsildar stamp, and photographs of documents sent over WhatsApp are not accepted. Fees run roughly ₹4,000 to ₹5,000 for a basic transfer, and an OTP is sent to the last registered buyer on record.

### "How do I verify a GMADA plot before buying?"

Visit the GMADA Estate Office at PUDA Bhawan, Sector 62, Mohali with the allotment letter or LOI and request a property statement. It shows instalments paid, pending dues, the current registered owner, and full transfer history. Verify this with the authority directly rather than relying on the seller's paperwork, which can look correct even after a plot has been cancelled.

### "What are the documents required to buy property in Mohali?"

Buying in Mohali requires the fard and jamabandi as title proof, the mother deed, a non-encumbrance certificate, property tax receipts from the Municipal Committee, and PSPCL electricity clearance. For GMADA plots, add the allotment letter, a GMADA property statement confirming no cancellation or dues, and permission to sell. After registration, apply for intkal and initiate the GMADA transfer.

## Tier 3: The problem queries

*Lowest competition, highest conversion. Each of these deserves its own page.*

### "Can a builder give possession without an occupancy certificate?"

No. Under RERA a promoter cannot offer possession or collect the final payment without a valid occupancy or completion certificate. Punjab RERA has held that possession offered without a completion certificate violates both PAPRA and RERA, and has awarded compensation for delay and mental agony. The Supreme Court has held such possession illegal.

### "What do I do if the builder is offering possession without an OC?"

Do not take possession. Once you are living in a building without an occupancy certificate, your leverage is gone and your legal position is weaker. Buyers retain the right to cancel the agreement, claim a refund, or seek compensation. Builders can face penalties including fines and cancellation of project registration. File with Punjab RERA at rera.punjab.gov.in.

### "How do I know if a plot has been cancelled by the builder?"

Ask the development authority directly rather than the seller. A plot can be cancelled at the builder's end for the seller's non-payment while the paperwork in the seller's hand still looks perfect. For GMADA plots, request a property statement from the Estate Office showing instalments paid, pending dues, and the current registered owner before making any payment.

### "What happens if the seller has not paid property tax?"

Outstanding property tax attaches to the property, not to the person who created the arrears. It becomes your liability the moment ownership transfers. On multi-floor buildings the risk is larger, because a building-level tax ID makes the back-dated liability collective across all floors. Request three years of receipts and confirm whether the tax ID is separate for your unit.

### "Is a power of attorney sale valid for property in India?"

A general power of attorney does not transfer ownership. The Supreme Court has held that GPA sales do not convey title, and ownership transfers only through a registered sale deed. If a seller proposes a GPA structure rather than a registered deed and cannot give a clear reason, treat it as a reason to walk away rather than a paperwork shortcut.

### "What happens if mutation is not done after registry?"

Registration still transfers ownership, but the revenue record continues to show the previous owner. That means the jamabandi will not carry your name, property tax records may stay in the seller's name, and any future buyer or lender running a check will find a mismatch. Apply for mutation within 30 days of registration.

### "Why is my registry showing super area instead of covered area?"

Registry should legally reflect the covered area as per revenue records. Builders often push buyers to register on super area because it inflates the apparent transaction value, and the buyer then pays stamp duty on the inflated figure. Check the area stated in the draft deed before execution, not on the registration date.

## Tier 4: Definition queries

*These get pulled into AI Overviews more reliably than any other type. Keep them short.*

### "What is an encumbrance certificate?"

An encumbrance certificate is an official record confirming that no loan, mortgage, lien, charge, or litigation is attached to a property. It is issued by the Sub-Registrar office, and in Punjab it is called a non-encumbrance certificate. It is the only document that reveals a hidden mortgage or pending legal claim, and it is required whether or not you are taking a home loan.

### "What is a mother deed in property?"

A mother deed traces the chain of ownership backward from the current seller through every previous owner to the original owner. Its purpose is to expose gaps in that chain, such as an heir who never signed a relinquishment or a family partition recorded on paper but never in the revenue record. It matters most on older and inherited properties.

### "What is the difference between a sale deed and a conveyance deed?"

A conveyance deed is the broader category: any document that transfers property rights from one party to another, including gift deeds and lease deeds. A sale deed is the specific type of conveyance deed used when property is transferred in exchange for payment. Every sale deed is a conveyance deed. Not every conveyance deed is a sale deed.

### "What is the difference between a completion certificate and an occupancy certificate?"

A completion certificate confirms that construction was completed as per the approved plan. An occupancy certificate is issued after final inspection and confirms the building is safe and legally habitable. The completion certificate is about the construction. The occupancy certificate is about whether you can legally live there. Do not take possession without the occupancy certificate.

### "What is an allotment letter in property?"

An allotment letter sets out the property details, price, and payment schedule agreed at the time of booking with a builder or development authority. It should match the agreement to sell exactly. Conditions do not belong in an allotment letter; every condition belongs in the agreement to sell, where both parties negotiate it.

### "What is the difference between an agreement to sell and a sale deed?"

An agreement to sell records the terms both parties have agreed and the promise to transfer the property in future. A sale deed executes that transfer. In Punjab the agreement to sell is called the bayana and is commonly on ₹4,000 stamp paper. Note that the Punjab and Haryana High Court has held that an agreement reciting delivery of possession attracts stamp duty as a conveyance.

### "What is CLU in Punjab?"

CLU stands for Change of Land Use, an order from PUDA permitting land to be used for a purpose other than its recorded classification. Agricultural land sold for residential use requires it. CLU and layout approval are two separate processes, and both must be complete. "Applied for approval" is not the same as "approved."

## Tier 5: NRI queries

### "What documents does an NRI need to buy property in India?"

The property-side checklist is identical to a resident purchase. Additional buyer-side requirements are FEMA compliance, payment routed through an NRO or NRE account, a PAN card, and a properly executed power of attorney if you are not present for registration. Residential property requires no RBI approval. Agricultural land, farmhouses, and plantation property do.

### "Can an NRI buy agricultural land in Punjab?"

No, not without RBI permission. Under FEMA, NRIs may purchase residential and commercial property in India without RBI approval, but agricultural land, farmhouses, and plantation property require specific RBI permission. Agricultural land can generally be acquired by an NRI only through inheritance.

### "How can an NRI verify property documents from abroad?"

Punjab land records are accessible online through the PLRS portal at jamabandi.punjab.gov.in, which allows verification of jamabandi records, mutation status, and fard requests remotely. RERA project status is verifiable at rera.punjab.gov.in. Encumbrance certificates and authority property statements require a local representative or a lawyer holding power of attorney.
